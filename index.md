# Privacy Policy for Smash The Wall

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Naoyuki Honda ("we") built Smash The Wall as a free app.
This page explains how information is handled when you use the app.

## 1. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information directly.

The game stores your hit count on your device only (using local storage).
This data is not sent to us or to any server.

## 2. Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 3. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page.
